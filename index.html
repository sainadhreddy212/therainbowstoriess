Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply as soon as we can.

How long does an invitation take?

Most e-cards are ready within a day or two, and video invites take a few days after we receive all your details. Share your event date and we'll plan the timeline around it.

What details do you need from me?

Names, date and time, venue with a map link, the functions you're inviting for, a few photos if you'd like them, your preferred language and a song.

Can I ask for changes after the preview?

Yes. We share a preview first so you can check spellings, timings and colours before we send the final file.

Which languages can you design in?

Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Hindi and English, or a bilingual invite that mixes two of them.

How will I receive my invitation?

On WhatsApp or email as an MP4 video, a PDF or an image, ready to forward to your guests straight away.

What is included in a wedding website?

A personal link with your names, a live countdown, your love story, every function with date, time and map, a photo gallery, guest RSVPs and an optional live stream link. We design it to match your invitation.

Can you match a design I saw somewhere?

Send us the reference and we'll create an original design in a similar mood, made around your names and story.
